# Complete shape-prior inversion and proof boundary

Let an observed target surface return be p=x+R*b with known |b_i|<=extent_i. Suppose R differs from a pure yaw U(theta) by operator norm at most tau. Then the XY difference from U(theta)*b is <=tau*R3D. Across a cell of halfwidth0.5degrees, replacing theta by its registered rational-axis midpoint moves any yaw-projected body point by <=RXY*0.00873, since 2sin(angle/2)<=angle and the rational axis direction error is below2e-9rad. Add8mm for centimeter quantization and nonnegative fitted delta. Thus every genuine point satisfies |n dot(p-x)| <= (extent_x+pad)*norm(n), and the analogous perpendicular inequality. Coefficient and radial upward rounding preserve containment. This is conditional on the body/tilt/point-group model; no label or latent heading classifier establishes those conditions.

For all p in G define a= max n dot p - A ceil(norm n), b= min n dot p + A ceil(norm n), and analogously c,d for the perpendicular vector. Empty [a,b] or[c,d] excludes only this cell. Otherwise these intervals define the rotated center rectangle. All cells and groups are unioned. Linear projection extrema are attained on the hull, so hull reduction loses no constraints. This complete union avoids replacing unknown yaw by a guessed most-likely heading.

At query q, clamp its orthogonal projections to the two intervals. If their displacement values are du,dv, the exact squared center distance is (du^2+dv^2)/(n dot n). The corresponding nearest center has rational world coordinates. Minimize the squared fraction across ALL compatible cells/groups, compare by integer cross products, then round square root down/up. This bounds the optimum distance for the declared finite OUTER cover. Any discretization, tilt padding and bounding-shape looseness remain; do not equate this with a raw-sensor-world optimum. Lower distance gives a conditional safe source deadline under the unchanged spherical body and future-motion envelope.

For joint reuse, one fitted episode score controls both c in sphere union and c in pose union. On that event, either deadline is valid, hence their maximum is valid for the intersection. The maximum of separate distance UPPER bounds is NOT a valid upper bound on the intersection distance: nearest witnesses may be different. No joint near-optimality claim is made without an intersection-feasible witness. Correlated views/model outputs require no independence claim in this joint event.

Development score is the minimum uniform projected-box expansion including the true center, plus the body score via a maximum, aggregated by episode. Missing/failed plans produce no new fact, rather than zero uncertainty. For a NEW independent prospective fit with scoring/design frozen beforehand and iid whole-episode law, max95 would support the previously stated perclass exceedance/confidence calculation. This already-inspected corpus does not meet those design-independence conditions, so report descriptive coverage only. Truth/grid diagnostics do not prove unseen obstacles, adversarial sender honesty, continuous physical bounds or useful ego control.
