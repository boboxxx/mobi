# Finite current-evidence proof repair

Frozen before new outcomes. Preserve the previous terrain score, calibration,
continuous pose prior, body-disc motion, unknown boundary and saved messages.
Use all six classes and both queries in three predetermined conditions:
(radius0, sigma0), (radius5mm, sigma1mm), (radius50mm, sigma10mm).
These are36 tasks, not independent risk trials. No actor truth seeds inference.

Import the existing old-only64k proof as a paid bootstrap cache. Revalidate old
excluded leaves against each fresh current message as before. Compare additional
repair budgets0,256,4096 node visits, independently from that same baseline, with
three timing repeats. Decoder, current update, context copy, queue construction,
repair and witness work are timed. Report all geometric and net results, including
losses from added work. No free best-budget choice after seeing outcomes.

Repair considers ALL retained leaves, including old unresolved leaves. Visit the
smallest contact lower bound first. Reuse current counts only for leaves already
checked in this same current update; otherwise evaluate the entire bounded message.
A failing exclusion leads to subdivision under the original geometric split rule.
Every64th repair visit probes the pose midpoint using a legal nominal realization
of the message (old center endpoints for balls, exact current exception endpoints).
An accepted pose is a conditional upper witness for message-compatible raw-score
states. Its contact bound uses the same abstract body-disc motion, not physical
mesh collision. Checks are at the two previously calibrated nested ray budgets.

The algorithm must preserve the old lower horizon, improve upper bounds only with
an independently rescored witness, and retain a complete partition. Independent
seven-plane audits recheck newly evaluated interval counts, inherited-current
provenance, all subdivisions/contact times and any witnesses. Budget0 must equal
the archived baseline. Larger budgets follow a deterministic prefix, so geometric
lower bounds cannot fall and upper bounds cannot rise. Net remaining time may fall.

This is a finite saved-observation study on sheng; no recurring loop or new CARLA
service. It does not calibrate physical sensing, inventory, motion, temporal risk
or establish a MobiCom contribution. Old independent audits remain immutable.
