`analyze.py` independently checks accepted complete prefixes, source pointclouds,
F/R dictionaries, historical preimages, whole-tile cover, positional propagation,
H/H+1 boundaries, received-only replay, sender caches and FIFO queues. Its two-host
outputs match bytewise. `witness_check.py` independently rechecks all84 prior
fixed candidates against the exact delivered historical supplements; its outputs
also match bytewise. `summary.json` and two PNG/PDF figures are generated by
`summarize.py`. The main study records actual sheng service durations. Three timing
repetitions are not three independent traffic scenes.

The normal timely metric keeps the existing50ms action-check grid and strict200ms
reserve. Coverage uses continuous permission intervals minus receiver CPU busy
intervals; this differs from older reports that start coverage at quantized ticks.
The root occupies source/link/receiver queues. Normal losses at27/28/29 do not
imply a wall-clock blackout for requests/replies. Sender caches insert only after
normal source generation completes. Source/receiver expensive proof work,
request/reply construction, encoding and modeled bidirectional wire delays are
charged. Lightweight commit/log/scheduler bookkeeping and physical stacks are
not a measured WCET; warm setup is separate. Facts restored after a deadline do
not retrospectively grant action.

`test_fixture_failure.log` is the retained initial fixture error: its default
radius exceeded a small synthetic domain. It was fixed before full measurement.
All10 final tests pass locally and on sheng. Unsolicited/stale/replayed replies,
corrupted and insufficient fragments, cache misses and exterior uncertainty are
covered. There is no scheduled experiment loop or new CARLA execution here.
