# Finite development experiment: body-support evidence lifetime

This protocol is frozen before the paid producer is run. The corpus and this body-support hypothesis have already been inspected in prior studies: this is development evaluation, not a new holdout or a prospective risk qualification. No thresholds, distributions or query placements are tuned by this experiment. No recurring research loop is created.

Input is the immutable prospective_expiry_20261003 stored stride-four XYZ test corpus: 360 planned episodes, including failed spawns, and 2076 captured frames. Use the fixed background map fitted earlier on causal_state calibration step-zero scans, the same known six-class body catalogue, fixed transforms/basis, and descriptive body-support slack zero. All retained components and their quantized points are included. Labels and actor truth never enter online processing. Known inventory, static sensor pose and fixed scene remain explicit assumptions.

For component G use the intersection of equal-radius disks centered at every quantized return, radius ceil(norm(extent)*1e6)+8000+slack micrometers. Use the union across components. The full convex hull is an exact reduction, because distance to a fixed candidate center is convex in the return position. Empty component sets are discarded only by an exact weighted variance certificate. An empty whole union revokes the episode; missing/precision refusal creates no new fact and retains earlier valid facts.

Query centers are (-6,0),(6,0) meters, query radius .75m, target body radius the unchanged 3D norm. Future center displacement is at most 5*t+1.5*t^2 meters. Lifetime cap is .5s. Source distance lower/upper gaps must be <=2 micrometers and expiry gaps <=1 microsecond. Floating native computations propose candidates only; exact Python integer/rational gates accept or refuse. Upper primal bounds prove closeness to the model optimum, not to all raw-compatible physical worlds. Test tangency, degenerate hulls, empty triple intersection, singleton triple, source age, revocation/refusal and paid setup before production.

Four actual checksum-protected zlib-6 formats: sparse active supporting halfspaces with at most two returns per nonempty component/query; all convex-hull returns; all component quantized returns; all stored sensor XYZ+transform. No cryptographic authenticity claim is made. Compact bounds are supported by an honest source/frontend and the body-support event; receiver checksum plus bound verification does not establish that a malicious sender reported all components. Raw baseline reencodes byte-identically the immutable parent RXYZ wire, registering its transport lineage and applying the NEW body-support model at the receiver. Parent centroid calibration does not transfer to this model.

Measure three complete source and receiver repetitions per frame/method. Source selection cost is inherited as the maximum original stride-four sampling measurement across parent methods, plus newly measured copy/frontend/geometry/encoding cost. Raw source copies and encodes stored XYZ; its receiver pays the same new frontend/full geometry. Active source pays full geometry/certificate generation. Hull source pays exact hull construction, and its receiver pays full geometry. Do not time cached proposals or precomputed certificates as production computation. Register native source/compiler/binary hashes; do not publish a macOS binary as a sheng artifact. Observed maxima are not WCET.

Common setup transfers full background provenance, catalogue/basis, transforms, model/transport registration and source hashes; measure actual wire bytes and three source/receiver repetitions. Warm assumes it was installed before episode t0. Cold releases and pays it at t0 ahead of all frames in the same three FIFO queues. Both modes are reported for all methods at 20 and 2 Mbps, 20ms propagation, fixed 200ms action+20ms clock reserve. Acquisition delay and immutable source timestamps are inherited. No arrival-time TTL refresh. Every planned episode has 16 decisions at 50ms steps and two queries, irrespective of capture success. Missing episodes are refusal, never denominator deletion.

Independent auditor imports none of the producer frontend, kernel, codec, engine, native solver. It reconstructs raw XYZ groups with a raster labeler, verifies hull equivalence, all-point feasible primal witnesses, integer halfspace dual/variance certificates, actual wire fields, timing maxima, FIFO costs and every decision. Offline exact rational truth membership, model-to-true-center isotropic oracle gaps and sampled future truth checks are diagnostics. No test-family calibrated guarantee, conditional grant risk, unseen mesh/intertick safety, unknown inventory completeness, live radio or ego-control claim.
