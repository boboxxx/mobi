# Primary reading and contribution boundary, 2026-10-04

These readings refine the problem statement; they are not reproduced baselines or evidence of a published MobiCom contribution. Existing earlier reading notes remain in the parent studies.

1. **HMS-SCP: Task-Oriented Multi-Scale Semantic Communication for V2X Cooperative Perception**, author arXiv v2, 18 August 2026. [Primary full HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.14603). Read abstract, introduction, methods/training, experimental setup and latency section IV-E. Multi-scale selected features and JSCC target cooperative 3D detection on OPV2V/DAIR-V2X. “Safety horizon” is used for far-field sensing range. Its inference profile and bandwidth analysis do not themselves define a timestamped worst-case last safe use of a received fact. Our interpretation is a different target metric, not proof that all related work lacks expiry reasoning. This is an author preprint submitted for possible IEEE publication, not verified venue acceptance. No reported latency or detection gain is imported as our measurement.

2. **Semantic Communication for Cooperative Perception using HARQ**, Sheng et al., arXiv:2409.09042v1. [Primary full HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2409.09042). Read system model, III-A/B/C/D and IV evaluation, particularly loss-difference-based semantic similarity, SimCRC threshold and SimHARQ feature combining/selection. The learned similarity threshold decides retransmission and the experiments assess perception/throughput. A source-age-dependent collision exclusion interval requires additional state/motion contracts; semantic similarity acceptance is not that certificate. Retransmission also cannot refresh a fact's acquisition epoch. We do not claim a like-for-like detection advantage over HARQ.

3. **Selective Language Communication for Cooperative Perception**, [OpenReview manuscript](https://openreview.net/pdf?id=MQMCpBv3fo), anonymous ACL submission in the indexed primary manuscript. Only indexed abstract/introduction were accessible; full PDF opening met a browser challenge. Those sections emphasize deployment-feature routing, coordinate messages and auditability. Record as a partial read; do not infer absence of temporal validity proofs or use its numerical gains. Plain-language auditable messages are not, by themselves, our novelty.

The full-point set model, convex hull reduction, supporting planes, enclosing-circle proposals and exact certificate arithmetic are established geometry. A credible systems contribution would need useful post-delivery lifetime with a measured benefit against SAME-INFORMATION full hull transport, robustly calibrated model adequacy, changing scenes and causal closed-loop control. Current source epochs and paid queues implement the measurement discipline. This finite reused-data experiment cannot establish the remaining claims.
