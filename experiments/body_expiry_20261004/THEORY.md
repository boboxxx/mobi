# What is and is not proved

Let E be the event that the target center at the immutable acquisition epoch lies in C = union_G intersection_{p in G} B(p,r). E is an explicit model adequacy event, not certified by the decoder. Taking all groups avoids oracle selection; taking all points per group does not prove any group's purity. Missing target evidence and unknown bodies are separate failure modes. This stage estimates no new probability for E.

Convex hull reduction is exact: if every hull vertex is within r of x, convexity of norm implies every interior point is too. For each convex intersection K and query q, any feasible x gives distance(K,q) <= norm(x-q). A supporting ball plane at arbitrary nonzero vector t gives t dot (x-c) <= r norm(t). With nonnegative weights w, put u=-sum(w*t). Then u dot (x-q) >= sum w [t dot (q-c)-r norm(t)]. Cauchy-Schwarz gives distance(K,q) >= max(0,N/norm(u)). Rounding numerator down and denominator up yields a valid integer lower bound. This dual certificate remains valid without the proposed x being feasible; feasibility is needed for the independent upper bound. In 2D a regular boundary optimum has at most two active ball normals; singular/empty cases require separate gates, rather than blindly applying that claim.

For weights w>=0, W=sum w and s=sum w*c, the variance identity gives every feasible x:
W^2*norm(x-s/W)^2 <= W^2*r^2 - [W*sum w*norm(c)^2 - norm(s)^2].
A strictly negative right side proves empty intersection. Zero confines the intersection to s/W, which must be checked against every constraint before being treated as a singleton. Three-circle pairwise intersection does not suffice. Exact source tests cover these cases.

Minimize component distances for the union, excluding only exactly proved empty components. If any component lacks a verified tight bracket, decline this new refinement. Expiry lower bound uses the strict inequality d_lower > body_radius+query_radius+5*t+1.5*t^2, with age measured from source, and is capped at .5s. The verifier returns the last integer microsecond strictly safe for that lower distance. Distance upper bound bounds the model optimum's age from above. Grant requires the stored source deadline to cover receiver now+220ms after all real encoded bytes and measured computation are paid.

On E and the displacement/body/coordinate contracts, the lower expiry is sound for the declared model. Tight numerical brackets make it near optimal for THAT ball-intersection model. They do not prove that the model is minimally conservative: the body sphere, grouping ambiguity, unknown unseen surfaces, isotropic motion and short cap can dominate. Therefore report the uncapped distance gap and the true-center oracle age gap, alongside coverage and strong baseline costs. Three timings do not establish a realtime deadline bound. Zero development failures do not establish a calibrated probability guarantee, and physical sampled traces do not prove continuous safety.
