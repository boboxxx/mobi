# Full-body integration gate, finite study

The previous 0.5 m disk monitor is not a whole-vehicle safety interface. This study first checks the geometry needed for a complete rectangular body and bounded translation/rotation, then exercises the receiver's explicit history, expiry and action-to-stop gate. No observation is labeled free merely because no actor was detected.

Body envelope: half-length 2 m, half-width 1 m, stated acceleration norm bound 3 m/s², yaw rate bound 0.2 rad/s and 3 cm radial registration allowance. At horizon H, use the rectangle swept by nominal measured velocity, dilated by 0.5*a*(H+clock)^2 + 2*body_radius*sin(min(pi,yaw_rate*(H+clock))/2) + pose allowance. Every potential obstacle center within its class outer radius plus reachable motion must be excluded by raw rays or a still-valid receiver-local prior. Required tiles include the half-diagonal and domain boundary guard. The two previous opaque-core profiles and 1 cm XYZ/origin/query boxes remain unchanged.

A prior is an external-free spatial region supported by a previously VERIFIED packet, at times before that packet expires. It is receiver-local state, not an assertion chosen by the sender. For initialization only, a free-body region may be an explicit instantaneous initial condition. Its timestamp does not silently grant future validity. This condition requires separate justification in a physical system. The new protocol must report bootstrap reliance and self-occlusion explicitly.

First examine all twelve saved initial clouds, speeds 0/0.5/1 m/s and horizons 0.2/0.4/0.6 s, with/without an instantaneous bootstrap. Heading comes from the documented RSU mount relative to the query, not obstacle labels. 216 outcomes including refusals. The no-ego saved clouds test enlarged action geometry only; they do not validate self-occlusion or driving. Direct all-ray coverage and compressed packet coverage must agree or report compression failure separately. Action-to-stop time includes acquisition/compute/transport age, 50 ms new command, 20 ms reaction, and braking with a stipulated lower bound 4 m/s². Keeping a 200 ms evidence horizon does NOT imply enough time to stop.

Subsequent live protocols must be frozen separately after this feasibility gate. Never silently shrink the body, relax physical bounds, remove the small-core class, use actor labels to authorize motion, or label stalled control as successful driving. No scheduled or recurring loop is created.
