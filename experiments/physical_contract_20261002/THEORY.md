# What incomplete observations can and cannot certify

The earlier result is conditional on W(I,A): all worlds consistent with received
information I AND a physical premise A. Its lower/upper bracket says nothing
unconditional about worlds excluded by a false A. A fast proof checker cannot
repair an invalid premise. SHA256, a receiver-owned parent and independently
recomputed reachable sets establish different properties from sensor/model truth.

This test instantiates the declared center as the actor bounding-box center,
matching prior CARLA evaluation code. A ray with a valid first return below the
probe plane has an empty prefix crossing that plane. A crossing strictly inside
the claimed solid horizontal disk contradicts that disk at this center. If even
the unchanged receiver's error- and whole-tile-debited rule excludes the tile
containing that real center, this is a robust operational counterexample to that
physical interpretation. It is not an observed collision or a counterexample to
the algebra conditional on a genuinely opaque disk.

The independent audit enumerates64 endpoint/origin error-box corner pairs and4
query-error corners for the decisive ray in every frame. The legacy interval
projection supplies the all-box conservative enclosure; independent corners
check its instantiated support. Tests with no contradictory ray do not prove a
continuous mesh core over all points, views, poses, animation or future motion.

An actor-labelled return farther than r_max from the declared center refutes that
outer envelope. A pair of actual returns separated by more than2*r_max refutes
EVERY alternative center for that radius, by the triangle inequality. This is
stronger than checking an enclosing bounding-box corner: a box corner need not
be a physical surface point. The audit computes the observed diameter via its
2D convex hull and all hull-vertex pairs.

There is an information limit without a minimum detectable structure or another
visibility premise. Finitely many ideal rays form finitely many1D segments in3D.
An open positive-volume task region contains a point not on these segments. Its
positive distance from this finite closed union permits a sufficiently small
opaque ball entirely between all rays. Adding it preserves every measured ray
while creating occupancy inside the task region. Therefore positive deterministic
free-space lifetime cannot follow from finite rays ALONE over an unrestricted
obstacle class. More samples can shrink the gap but do not remove this logical
requirement. Empty initial occupancy known independently changes the premise;
then motion bounds can buy time, as already recognized by the historical methods.

The implementation response here is deliberately limited. `ContractGate` accepts
only locally pinned analysis/audit bytes and a configured full allowed population.
It distinguishes refutation from finite non-refutation and retains a conditional
horizon for diagnostics. It never treats observed actor labels as the population
of potential hidden actors. Changed radii or unseen blueprints remain unverified.
`OperationalBoundary` cannot grant physical actions from this negative-evidence
registry. There is currently no positive physical calibration certificate. This
is a boundary against misuse, not a completed adaptive controller.

The unresolved model problem is now specific: establish an appropriate shape/
pose/height/observation contract for the intended population, or replace the
single-plane solid-disk abstraction with shape-aware3D evidence and an explicit
residual-risk calibration. Selecting a smaller radius from the same48 frames and
calling those frames validation would not solve it. Dynamic and independently
held-out tests must follow the replacement model, not precede its definition.
