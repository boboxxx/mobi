# Post-search finite candidate transfer and raw-data diagnostic

Declared after seeing the frozen36 receiver-only searches. No initial search is
rerun or retuned. For each class, pool every successive-best accepted pose from
all six same-class task searches, deduplicated by exact float bytes in task and
evaluation order. Evaluate this finite pool on every one of that class's six
messages. These are offline reused proposals, not fresh independent observations
or a free online cross-task oracle. Select the closest accepted pose for each
query only after exact score checks against that target message's legal nominal
realization. Also retain the best original-search candidate as an alternative
if its query-specific optimizer found a closer accepted state.

Then, in the auditor only, load the actual saved current raw packet and rescore
the identical fixed candidate pool. Actual data cannot add candidates, change
optimization or repair a failed receiver-only claim. Report separate nominal
message and actual-raw witnesses. Exact-radius cases must agree identically.
Audit every pool candidate on all rays with independent world-plane intersections,
and verify every chosen contact upper bound using the high-precision reference.

An actual-raw accepted witness with upper <=240ms proves that the *frozen score
and body-disc model* cannot support positive remainder even with zero coding,
wire and computation beyond the existing20ms propagation+20ms clock+200ms reserve.
It does not prove actual physical danger. If a nominal witness fails under actual
raw data, full coordinates distinguish that specific state; this does not prove
that transmitting them eliminates all short-horizon states. Differences between
heuristic upper witnesses do not equal the information-theoretic compression loss.

Keep all task rows, candidates, scores and failures. Do not charge any offline
search or transfer as a free improvement to published online experiments.
