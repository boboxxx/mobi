1. **Wu et al., Goal-oriented Semantic Communications for Robotic Waypoint
Transmission: The Value and Age of Information Approach**, IEEE TWC accepted
manuscript, arXiv v2, November2024.
[Primary full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2312.13182v2).
Read abstract, introduction, II-A–D and IV-A2. It uses waypoint tracking MSE,
command generation/repetition and AoI/VoI queue ordering. II-C3 assumes ideal
uplink feedback; IV-A2 omits data-processing time. Our deduction: useful
communication must account for verification/queue costs, and mean tracking
error alone cannot justify a worst-case hidden-obstacle validity certificate.
This is a difference in evaluated problem, not proof of an unoccupied niche.
We do not reproduce its DRL/protocol or claim a direct experimental comparison.

2. **Khan et al., Goal-Oriented Communication and Control Co-Design via Semantic
Push-Pull in Industrial IoT**, arXiv2609.21566v1, September18 2026.
[Primary full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.21566v1).
Read abstract/introduction and II-B, III-A–D, IV-A–B. A predictor innovation
ratio and age-dependent decay guide pushes/pulls under shared resources;
its downlink is capacity-limited and error-free. Our deduction: innovation
confidence does not itself bound invisible class-center occupancy or certify
an action expiry. Semantic confidence/push-pull scheduling is already a nearby
idea, so renaming it as evidence scheduling would not establish novelty.
This is a recent preprint, not verified peer-reviewed high-quality evidence.

Game-Theoretic Active Perception**, RSS2021.
[Official full paper](https://www.roboticsproceedings.org/rss17/p066.pdf).
